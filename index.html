Communications strategist Storyteller Problem-solver

Loves connecting dots, people and ideas.

My journey has taken me from London to Liverpool, Dakar to Cairo, Johannesburg and Lagos, creating meaningful connections and delivering results through words, strategy, and engaging campaigns (including events).

With 10 years' experience in strategic communications and marketing, I help industry leaders and organisations achieve clarity and position themselves for success. My background spans UK higher education, private capital and development finance, central government and the charity sector.

I work with industry leaders to craft compelling narratives and design campaigns that increase engagement. From managing global conference communications to producing multimedia campaigns and hosting podcasts with private capital decision-makers, I bring creativity, vision and rigour to every project.

As Co-Founder (Co-Sister) of a West African doughnut brand (Puff Puff Ministry) serving clients like Google, Netflix and Spotify, I understand how authentic storytelling builds communities and drives engagement.

If you're looking for a host/moderator for your podcast/event, or a producer for your corporate conference, I am open to connecting and sharing ideas. Email me.
